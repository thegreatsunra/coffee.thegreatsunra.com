Coffee Water Mixer

How the numbers are calculated

Hardness and alkalinity are both expressed as ppm (parts per million) as CaCO3: the amount of calcium carbonate that would have the same effect. That puts both minerals on one scale, so targets and concentrate strengths can be compared directly.

Salt for a concentrate = strength × bottle total ÷ 1,000,000 × (salt molar mass ÷ CaCO3 molar mass). Baking soda is multiplied by 2, because two bicarbonates equal one CaCO3.

Batch weight = fluid ounces × 29.5735 mL per fl oz × 1 g/mL. Water at room temperature is about 0.998 g/mL, so this runs about 0.2% high.

Concentrate to add = batch weight × target ÷ concentrate strength.
